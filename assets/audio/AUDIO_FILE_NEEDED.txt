⚠️ IMPORTANT: MISSING AUDIO FILE ⚠️

You need to add the "6-7" audio track for the game to work!

Required File:
  📁 six_seven_v1.mp3

Where to place it:
  📂 assets/audio/six_seven_v1.mp3

File Requirements:
  - Duration: ~30 seconds
  - Format: MP3
  - Sample Rate: 44.1kHz or higher
  - Quality: 128kbps or higher

How to Get the Audio:
  1. Download a royalty-free remix of "6-7" audio
  2. OR create your own remix
  3. OR use a placeholder track for testing

IMPORTANT - Audio Licensing:
  ⚠️ The original "6-7" TikTok audio is COPYRIGHTED!

  If publishing this game, you MUST:
  - Use a royalty-free remix
  - Get permission from the original creator
  - Partner with the TikTok creator for revenue share

Beat Timing:
  The current beat timing data is configured for:
  - BPM: 120
  - Duration: 30 seconds
  - 60 beats total

  If your audio file has a different BPM or structure, you'll need to:
  1. Open your audio in Audacity
  2. Find the exact beat times (in milliseconds)
  3. Update: assets/audio/beat_timings/six_seven_v1.json

Testing Without Audio:
  You can test the game mechanics using ANY 30-second MP3 file!
  Just name it "six_seven_v1.mp3" and place it here.
