**⚡ Instant Action** – Matches start in seconds with 2+ players. No tutorials, no waiting—just pure rhythm chaos.

**🎯 Unforgiving Precision** – Hit PERFECT (±150ms) for maximum points, but one MISS and you're eliminated. Spectate as a ghost and watch the battle unfold.

**🏆 Competitive Progression** – Earn XP with every match and climb five Rizz Ranks: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Certified 6-7, and the legendary Meme Lord. Track your wins, perfect hits, and accuracy with the `/stats` command.

**🎵 Perfectly Synced Audio** – Experience the iconic "Six... Seven" voice with crystal-clear beat detection and satisfying sound effects on every hit.

## **Controls Breakdown**

**Desktop:**
- **WASD** – Move around the arena
- **SPACE** – Jump
- **6 or 7 Keys** – Jump to the platform that matches the beat (listen carefully!)

**Mobile:**
- **Virtual Joystick** – Move your character
- **On-screen Buttons** – Tap **6** or **7** when the platform lights up

**How to Win:**
1. Listen to the audio cue ("Six!" or "Seven!")
2. Watch for the platform indicator to light up
3. Press the matching key (6 or 7) right on the beat
4. Survive 6 rounds without missing or pressing wrong
5. Highest score wins!

Master the timing, dominate the meme. Let's goooo! 🚀
